The source construction exactly reproduces 6,144 finite Fourier cells for targets inside [0,1], but its positive rescaled square-root observable is nonnegative. A signed target sin(x) has normalized L2 error at least 0.5 against every nonnegative output; the negative constant control has lower bound 0.25. The source range condition is therefore essential.
