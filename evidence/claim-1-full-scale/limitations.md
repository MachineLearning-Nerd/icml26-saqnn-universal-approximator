# Limitations and deviations

Finite computation cannot enumerate the theorem's uncountable L2 target
class. Verification of the universal quantifier therefore depends on the
paper's cited density theorem as well as the machine-checked SAQNN reduction.
The Monte Carlo values use deterministic seeds and 95% standard-error
intervals, but they are supporting evidence rather than the logical proof.
No noisy-hardware, optimizer, or training-dynamics claim was attempted.
