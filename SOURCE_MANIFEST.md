## Reproduction provenance

The source inputs are retained locally and verified by SHA-256 before the
claim producers run. The accepted source has no author executable, so every
producer in `repro/src/` is an independent clean-room implementation. The
formal environment is pinned by `uv.lock` and `.python-version`.
