# Claim 5 method

One checker simulates the complete state-preparation, diagonal
spectrum-selection, and readout circuit for Fourier modes in one and two
dimensions and Chebyshev modes `T_1` through `T_7`. A second regression
computes Fourier and Chebyshev approximation curves for periodic and
non-periodic targets. Basis distinctness and a wrong-basis comparison serve as
controls.
