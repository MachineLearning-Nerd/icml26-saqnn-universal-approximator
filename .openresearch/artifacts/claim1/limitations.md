# Claim 1 limitations and deviations

- Finite computation cannot enumerate an uncountable L2 function class.
- The universal quantifier is justified by the source-cited Fourier-density
  theorem plus a machine-checked constructive reduction. The numerical sweeps
  are stress tests of that reduction, not a replacement for density.
- Monte-Carlo L2 estimates are reported with uncertainty and are not promoted
  to exact proof statements.
- The clean-room statevector calculation models ideal gates. Hardware noise,
  trainability, optimizer convergence, and sample complexity are outside
  Theorem 1 and are not evaluated.
- No author executable was available in the accepted source.
